## The Problem

Invoices arrive as documents, and turning them into structured data took **manual intervention**. Extraction success sat at **35%**, so most documents still needed a person.

## The Approach

Each document becomes a **job with tracked state**, so every invoice can be followed from upload to result. **OCR** recognizes the text and layout, an **LLM** extracts structured fields from it, and **IRN / e-invoice** compliance data is parsed and checked. Failed jobs are **retried with backoff** instead of being handled by hand.

## My Contribution

I **designed and built** the pipeline **end to end**: **job-state management**, **retry/backoff**, **structured extraction** and **compliance parsing**.

The same OCR stack also powers **Aadhaar document masking**. It masks **PDF, JPEG and PNG** documents using **PDF redaction** with an **OCR fallback**, and is exposed through **authenticated APIs** with **concurrent background processing**.

## The Outcome

Extraction success rose from **35% to 85%+**, and **manual intervention dropped**.
