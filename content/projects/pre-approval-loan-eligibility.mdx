## The Problem

The signals behind a **lending decision** live in many places: **ERP data**, **ledgers** provided by hand, **sales history**, **CIBIL** and **GSTIN**. Each arrives in a **different shape**, and none of them is a decision on its own.

## The Approach

An **aggregator** normalizes and consolidates every source into **one view**. **Business rules** and **statistical checks** then score creditworthiness, and the result is a **data-driven pre-approval** outcome.

## My Contribution

I **architected and developed** the eligibility system, including the aggregation of **ERP data**, **manual ledgers**, **sales history**, **CIBIL**, **GSTIN** and other parameters into a **single assessment** and decision workflow.

## The Outcome

Lending decisions now come from a **consolidated, data-driven credit assessment** rather than from separate sources reviewed one at a time.
